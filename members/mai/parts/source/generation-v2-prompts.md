# Mai generation v2

- Every visible rig layer is newly generated; no reference-image crop is used as an authored part.
- Hair outputs must contain only white/light-gray hair and its dark outline/shading.
- Skin, scalp, ears, face, clothes, guides, and text are forbidden in hair source images.
- Rear, upper, and front hair are separate drawings with broad hidden roots and distinct silhouettes.
- Pure green source backgrounds are removed only as a technical alpha conversion after generation.
